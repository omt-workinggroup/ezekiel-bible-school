The curriculum

“Presenting every believer mature in Christ.” Colossians 1:28. Six levels of study, every course listed semester by semester.

Knowledge that becomes conviction, character and service.

The institute is designed to move students beyond the accumulation of biblical information toward integrated Christian formation. The goal is knowledge that becomes conviction, character, competence, faithful service and multiplication.

Level 01 Undergraduate

Certificate in Christian Foundations

Length
1 year
Structure
2 semesters

Establish new and growing believers in the fundamental truths of Christianity.

“I know the foundations of the faith.”

Semester 14 courses
  • CF 101 The Gospel and SalvationGospel, sin, repentance, faith, grace, justification, regeneration, new birth, adoption, eternal life, assurance
  • CF 102 The Person of Jesus ChristDeity and humanity, incarnation, virgin birth, sinlessness, death, resurrection, ascension, lordship
  • CF 103 The Holy SpiritPerson and deity, indwelling, regeneration, sanctification, filling, gifts, fruit, guidance, witness
  • CF 104 Introduction to the BibleInspiration, authority, canon, Old and New Testaments, covenants, revelation, illumination, reading Scripture
Semester 24 courses
  • CF 105 Christian Identity and SonshipNew creation, identity in Christ, righteousness, adoption, sonship, union with Christ, freedom
  • CF 106 The Christian LifeFaith, prayer, worship, holiness, love, forgiveness, humility, wisdom, stewardship
  • CF 107 Introduction to Spiritual GrowthNew birth, infancy, growth, maturity, character, spiritual disciplines, stability, responsibility
  • CF 108 Introduction to Christian ServiceCalling, service, gifts, faithfulness, character, servanthood, evangelism, discipleship

Level 02 Undergraduate

Diploma in Christian Ministry & Biblical Studies

Length
1 year
Structure
2 semesters

Move students from foundational Christianity into responsible biblical understanding and practical ministry.

“I understand Scripture and can practise the Christian life.”

Semester 14 courses
  • DM 201 Principles of Biblical InterpretationHistorical, literary, immediate and canonical context, genre, translation, exegesis, eisegesis, application
  • DM 202 Biblical TheologyCreation, fall, promise, covenant, kingdom, Messiah, redemption, New Covenant, church, new creation
  • DM 203 Spiritual MaturityNēpios, paidion, neaniskos, patēr; spiritual understanding, character, stability, responsibility, reproduction
  • DM 204 Knowing GodEidō, ginōskō, gnōsis, epignōsis; knowledge of God, revelation, fellowship, experiential knowledge
Semester 24 courses
  • DM 205 Introduction to Christian DoctrineThe doctrines of God, Christ, the Holy Spirit, salvation, the church and last things
  • DM 206 Prayer and Spiritual LifeFellowship with God, petition, thanksgiving, intercession, corporate prayer, fasting, spiritual warfare
  • DM 207 Evangelism and DiscipleshipGreat Commission, gospel presentation, personal evangelism, follow-up, discipleship, mentoring, multiplication
  • DM 208 Ministry Practicum IBible study, evangelism, prayer leadership, discipleship, supervised ministry reflection

Ministry practicum & portfolio

Students demonstrate ministry competence, not just knowledge, through:

  • Bible-study projects
  • Sermon and teaching practicum
  • Evangelism
  • Prayer leadership
  • Discipleship
  • A ministry problem-solving project
  • A final portfolio showing knowledge, character, skill, service and fruit

Programme progression

  1. Certificate I know the foundations of the faith.
  2. Diploma I understand Scripture and can practise the Christian life.
  3. Bachelor I can interpret Scripture, understand theology and serve effectively.
  4. Master I can critically investigate, articulate and apply theological truth.
  5. DMin I can use theological knowledge and research to transform ministry practice.
  6. PhD I can conduct original research and contribute new knowledge.

The graduate we pray for

I know Christ, understand His Word, understand who I am in Christ, live according to His character, interpret Scripture responsibly, discern sound doctrine, pray effectively, disciple others, serve according to my calling, and contribute faithfully to the mission and health of the local church.
