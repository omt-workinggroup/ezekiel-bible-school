About the institute

Ezekiel Bible Institute began in 2001 with eleven students in a borrowed church hall. Today we teach around 400 students a year from Christian Service Church in East Legon, Accra, and online.

What we are for

We exist to train people who will teach the Bible faithfully and care for the church well. Most of our graduates go on to serve in local congregations across Ghana and West Africa; others work in mission, chaplaincy, schools and the workplace around the world.

We are evangelical and interdenominational. Students come from Anglican, Baptist, Pentecostal, Charismatic, Methodist and independent churches, and from many countries. All of them affirm our statement of faith.

How we teach

Classes are small and you will be expected to read a lot, write regularly and explain a passage to people who did not come to hear a lecture.

Scripture
Every student reads the whole Bible in their first year and takes at least one term of Greek.
Formation
Groups of eight meet weekly with a tutor to pray and talk honestly.
Practice
From the second term you serve each week with a partner church in Accra.
