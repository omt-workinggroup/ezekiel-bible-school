Admissions

We look for people who are already serving in their church and want to be better equipped for it. You don’t need a theology background.

How to apply

  1. Enquire

    We send a prospectus and introduce you to an admissions tutor.

  2. Apply

    A short form, a one-page testimony and your pastor’s letter.

  3. Interview

    Thirty minutes, in person or by video call.

  4. Accept your offer

    Bursary decisions come with your offer, so you know the full cost first.

Fees and bursaries

Fees are listed with each programme and are charged per year. Campus fees include books and lunch; accommodation is separate.

68% of current students receive a bursary. Most awards cover between a quarter and three quarters of fees, and are based on household income and church support.

Accommodation

Shared rooms on campus cost $90 a month with meals. We have six flats for married students, allocated in order of acceptance.

Questions

Can I study online from another country?

Yes. Lectures are recorded and seminars run at two times each week.

Do I need to have studied theology before?

No. The certificate and diploma programmes start from the beginning.

Can I transfer credit from another college?

Usually, for up to a third of a programme. Send your transcript with your application and the Dean will review it.

Can I work while I study?

Many online and part-time students do. Full-time campus study leaves room for around ten hours of work a week.
