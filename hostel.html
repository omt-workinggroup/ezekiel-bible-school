Hostel accommodation

A safe, quiet place to live on the grounds of Christian Service Church in East Legon, Accra, a short walk from your classes and the chapel.

Live where you learn

Most full-time campus students live in the hostel. You eat, pray and study alongside classmates from across Ghana and from other countries, and the first lecture of the day is minutes away.

Rooms are shared by two to four students, with separate wings for men and women.

Hostel questions

Who can stay in the hostel?

Any full-time campus student, from Ghana or abroad. Rooms are allocated in order of acceptance, so apply early if you need one.

Are men and women housed separately?

Yes. The hostel has separate wings for men and women, each with its own warden.

What should I bring?

Bedding, towels, a reading lamp and personal items. Beds, mattresses, desks and wardrobes are provided.

Can I stay during the holidays?

International students can stay over the short breaks by arrangement with the hostel office.
