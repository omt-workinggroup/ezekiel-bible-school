Programmes

Six programmes, from a twelve-week evening course to a three-year degree. Every one can be started in January or September.

Programme Length Mode Per year
Certificate in Biblical StudiesOld and New Testament survey, Christian doctrine, prayer and the spiritual disciplines. 1 year Campus, online $1,200
Diploma in Pastoral MinistryPreaching, pastoral care and church leadership, with a supervised placement. 2 years Campus $1,850
Bachelor of TheologyGreek and Hebrew, systematic theology, church history and hermeneutics. 3 years Campus, online $2,400
Diploma in MissionsCross-cultural ministry and church planting. Includes a three-week field trip. 18 months Online $1,500
Certificate in Biblical CounsellingCare for individuals and families, grounded in Scripture. 6 months Campus, online $650
Evening School of DiscipleshipA short course for church members. Tuesday evenings, no entry requirements. 12 weeks Online $120

Campus fees include books and lunch. Online students pay 20% less and buy their own textbooks.

Entry requirements

  • A recommendation from your pastor or church leader
  • Secondary school completion for diploma and degree programmes
  • English at a level where you can read academic texts comfortably

No previous theological study is needed. If you have studied elsewhere, you may be able to transfer up to a third of a programme.

Studying online

Online students follow the same syllabus and are taught by the same faculty. Lectures are recorded; seminars run twice a week at different times so that most time zones are covered.

You will need a reliable internet connection and about 15 hours a week for a full-time load.
