Programmes

Six levels of study, from a one-year Certificate in Christian Foundations to a PhD in Biblical & Theological Studies, taught at Christian Service Church in East Legon, Accra.

Programme Length Structure Stage
Level 1Certificate in Christian FoundationsEstablish new and growing believers in the fundamental truths of Christianity. 1 year 2 semesters Undergraduate
Level 2Diploma in Christian Ministry & Biblical StudiesMove students from foundational Christianity into responsible biblical understanding and practical ministry. 1 year 2 semesters Undergraduate
Level 3Bachelor of Theology / Christian MinistryEquip students to interpret Scripture responsibly, understand doctrine, develop maturity and serve effectively. 3 years 6 semesters Undergraduate
Level 4Master of Theology / Christian MinistryMove students into advanced critical investigation, theological articulation, research and application. 2 years 4 semesters Postgraduate
Level 5Doctor of Ministry (DMin)A professional doctorate integrating theological research with ministry transformation and practical solutions. 3 years 6 semesters Postgraduate
Level 6PhD in Biblical & Theological StudiesPrepare scholars capable of advanced biblical and theological research and an original contribution to knowledge. 3–5 years Research-led Postgraduate

Every course, semester by semester, is on the curriculum page. Contact admissions for current fees; hostel rooms are charged separately.

Entry requirements

  • A recommendation from your pastor or church leader, for every level
  • Certificate: open to new and growing believers
  • Diploma and Bachelor: WASSCE or an equivalent secondary qualification
  • Master: a bachelor’s degree, usually in theology or ministry
  • DMin: a master’s degree and active ministry experience
  • PhD: a master’s degree with a research component

International qualifications are accepted. Teaching is in English.

Ministry practicum

At every level, students show what they can do, not just what they know. Practicum work includes:

  • Bible-study projects
  • Sermon and teaching practicum
  • Evangelism
  • Prayer leadership
  • Discipleship
  • A ministry problem-solving project
  • A final portfolio showing knowledge, character, skill, service and fruit
