Our mission

Forming leaders who bring life where there was none

Like the prophet in the valley of dry bones, we believe God’s Word spoken in faith brings renewal. For over two decades we have trained pastors, missionaries, teachers and marketplace leaders to handle Scripture with care and serve with compassion.

  • Christ-centred, Bible-saturated curriculum
  • Mentoring in small formation groups
  • Ministry placements in local churches every term
  • Affordable fees with scholarships for every programme

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a church recommendation to apply?

Yes. We ask for a short letter from your pastor or a church leader who knows you well.

Can I study online from outside the country?

Absolutely. Our online students join from over 40 nations. Lectures are recorded and live sessions are scheduled across time zones.

Are scholarships available?

Every programme has partial scholarships based on need and ministry involvement. Indicate your interest on the application form.

Is the school denominational?

We are evangelical and interdenominational, welcoming students from many church traditions who affirm our statement of faith.

Is accommodation provided?

On-campus students can choose from shared hostel rooms with meals included. Limited family housing is also available.
